Skyworth Group

Founded in 1988, Skyworth is a globally competitive backbone enterprise in smart home appliances and information technology, ranked 380th on the 2025 China Top 500 Enterprises list.

The Group operates four major business segments: Smart Home Appliances, Smart System Technology, New Energy, and Modern Services.

Core activities: R&D, manufacturing and sales of smart TVs, refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners, kitchen appliances, personal-care small appliances, and Coocaa internet connectivity services; digital smart set-top boxes, smart gateways, smart mesh networking, spatial computing (VR/MR/AI glasses/AR), automotive smart display assemblies, industrial display modules (drones / EV charging piles, etc.), and IoT solutions; new-energy photovoltaics, energy storage, modules and inverters; park property management, logistics services, home-appliance maintenance value-added services, and venture investment.

Xiaowei Health Tech

Xiaowei Health Technology is a high-tech medical device and service company under the publicly listed Skyworth Group, dedicated to the integrated R&D, production and marketing of smart wearable devices, acoustic and hearing-health systems. The company operates two flagship brands — [SKYWORTH] and [BigSound] — focusing on AI Chinese hearing aids, health smartwatches, kids' phone watches, Bluetooth earphones and other wearable products.

The company brings together a senior, cross-disciplinary team of leading domestic and international experts in smart hardware, acoustics and audiology, algorithms, and consumer-electronics brand marketing with 20 years of industry experience, and holds nearly a hundred patents in hearing aids, acoustics, and health smart wearables. Guided by a brand mission of building "healthy, smart" products with a people-first philosophy, Xiaowei Health leverages technology to power proactive wellness, helping people transform their lifestyles so everyone can enjoy a healthy, beautiful life.

The company focuses on frontier R&D in health smart wearables, spanning cardio-cerebral health monitoring, early warning and intervention, hearing assistance, hearing acoustics, and artificial intelligence. By aggregating resources from the Tencent Tianlai Lab, Skyworth's R&D and supply-chain capabilities, and Ping An Health services, Xiaowei Health delivers wearable health-system solutions for health-conscious users and people with hearing loss, creating a healthy and beautiful life for every user.

SKYWORTH kids' phone watches and health early-warning watches have passed review by China Mobile, China Telecom and China Unicom at the group level, and have been added to their national marketing catalogs. Related smart wearable products and services are widely deployed across smart campuses, education, senior care, health community management, and home photovoltaic systems.

Facing a Chinese hearing-aid market long monopolized by foreign capital in technology, products and channels, "BigSound" pioneered the Chinese hearing aid concept, dedicated to developing hearing-aid products better suited to Chinese-language contexts — with more natural sound and better fit for Chinese daily-life scenarios. Committed to breaking the blockade and monopoly of the foreign Big Five hearing-aid groups, BigSound provides Chinese users with comparable professional medical-grade hearing aids, delivering stronger performance, superior listening experience, and better value. The brand aims to build China's national hearing-aid brand, drive hearing-aid adoption, benefit people with hearing loss, and safeguard the health of older adults.
