Low Awareness

If hearing-impaired individuals receive timely hearing aid fitting and rehabilitation services, the vast majority can have their hearing compensated and participate in social activities like normal-hearing people, improving their quality of life

User Low Awareness

  • 67% of seniors don't know their hearing has declined or been lost
  • Only 7.4% of ear disease patients in China have received hearing tests
  • Only 8% of hearing-impaired seniors over 65 received hearing tests in the past year

Government Low Awareness

  • Child subsidies are well-established, but senior subsidies only exist in Beijing/Shanghai/Guangdong/Fujian
  • WHO 2017 Global Deafness Prevention Resolution requires otology to extend to grassroots and communities, but China's progress is not ideal
  • WHO requires hearing aid adoption rate to reach 50% by 2030; China still has a huge gap

Favorable Policies

The state has included hearing aids in the key development track of rehabilitation assistive devices, with 14 ministries jointly issuing a special three-year action plan, providing targeted policy support across the entire chain — core technology research, industrial platform building, financial credit support, medical insurance linkage, first-set certification, and talent training system

01

Industrial Planning Support

14 departments jointly issued the "Three-Year Action Plan for Capacity Expansion and Quality Improvement of Rehabilitation Assistive Devices Industry (2026-2028)", listing hearing aids as one of the 6-8 core sub-industries to be strengthened, supporting enterprises to break through key technologies and list financing, with the goal of exceeding 500 above-scale rehabilitation assistive enterprises by 2028.

02

Hearing Health Action

In 2024, the "Elderly Hearing Health Promotion Action (2024-2027)" was launched to advance hearing screening, intervention, and science popularization across all age groups, improving industry service capacity.

03

Centralized Procurement Price Reduction

National subsidy policies have been introduced, significantly reducing hearing reconstruction costs for hearing-impaired groups and driving the trend toward universal access to hearing aids and related assistive devices.

04

National Standard Upgrade

Starting July 1, 2026, the new national safety standard for hearing aids will be officially implemented, unifying internationally aligned technical testing requirements, regulating industry access, and ensuring product quality and safety.

Raising National Hearing Health Awareness

With the continuous rollout of National Ear Care Day science popularization and the widespread adoption of CDC ear health guidelines, from active hearing screening by seniors to attention to noise-induced hearing loss among young people, national hearing health awareness is undergoing a key shift from "passive neglect" to "active intervention"
